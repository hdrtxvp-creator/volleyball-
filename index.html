Sides
How many teams 1

Left to itself the app aims for sides of about six and counts them off the players coming: 15 makes three fives, 19 makes three sixes with the spare player added to one side, 24 makes four sixes. Turn the switch off to set the number yourself. Either way everyone available plays, and the sides are evened out by ranking — the 4s and 5s get split up, never stacked on one side.
